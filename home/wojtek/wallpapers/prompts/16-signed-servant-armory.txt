SCENE 16 — SIGNED SERVANT ARMORY

Original visual language: V Rising gameplay in its stylized isometric Gothic
rendering, with Castle Heart domain planning, servant workstations, coffins,
blood essence and original fully clothed vampire characters.

Use three major props. A Forgejo-shaped source lectern receives one "push" and
summons a single Woodpecker servant. Its compact two-chamber CI forge first runs
one red-or-green test, then builds the expedition weapon once and binds a short
SBOM inventory to it. The Castle Heart seal signs that same artifact before a
servant stores it in the third prop: one registry chest by immutable digest. An
unsigned weapon marked "latest" is rejected into the smelter. The overseer is
tense at the red test, focused during repair and calm only when the artifact is
signed.

The complete visible flow is only Forgejo push -> Woodpecker test plus
build/SBOM -> sign -> registry. Use no deployment environments, Flux,
Kubernetes, giant CI interface or supply-chain logo wall.

Exactly one crossover: a yellow rubber duck wears a tiny recognizable Stark
helmet. Gather vampire overseer, servants, three-chamber line, seal, signed
chest and duck naturally to the right of centre. Authentic V Rising workshops,
floor tiles and unlit rooms form one uninterrupted isometric domain across the
entire panorama.
