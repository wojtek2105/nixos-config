SCENE 20 — DEPENDENCY DEVIL

Original visual language: unmistakable Chainsaw Man anime/manga cinematic with
canonical Makima, Denji and Power, their exact faces, hair, eyes, default Public

urban grime, dark comedy and dynamic camera. No generic anime substitutes,
sexualized redesign or graphic gore.

Beneath a dead overpass, a Dependency Devil is physically built from one
package chain. One combined lockfile/SBOM map exposes a poisoned transitive
link to a compact scanner. Makima identifies it, Denji replaces only that link
and Power holds the healthy graph. The same scanner changes from red to green
after the rebuild, visibly closing the incident. The flow is only dependency
map -> scan -> patch/rebuild -> rescan. Use no ecosystem logo wall.

Exactly one crossover: a small worn poster of Frieren is visible inside an
abandoned kiosk. Gather all three characters, complete action, devil chain,
lockfile, SBOM and poster naturally to the right of centre. The overpass
structure and black city recesses form one continuous anime panorama with
coherent perspective, ambient occlusion and practical light.
