SCENE 17 — FILESYSTEMS AT BLACK FJORD

Original visual language: unmistakable Valheim — low-poly forms, painterly
textures, Nordic survival scale, rough timber, fog and firelight, upgraded with
richer material detail but never photorealistic. Use original fully clothed
Viking characters and authentic game-world tools.

At a working black fjord port, compress the comparison into three props. First,
one exchange ferry has two holds: FAT32 stopped by a small "4 GiB" gate and
exFAT carrying a larger cross-platform chest. Second, one compatibility bench
opens locked NTFS and sealed APFS imports for migration, never as the central
Linux foundation. Third, one open Linux storage hall has three compact bays:
robust ext4 for ordinary local storage, Btrfs with one snapshot wheel for a
workstation, and mirrored ZFS with checksum plus one scrub wheel for server
data. Track one chest from import through verification into its chosen bay; do
not show every format as a separate building.

The visual opinion is clear but technically fair: Linux offers the open path
and the widest control; FAT/exFAT are exchange formats, and proprietary NTFS or
APFS remain compatibility inputs rather than being mocked with false failures.
Use names only on real chests or workshop plates, never as giant logos.

Exactly one crossover: Frieren's staff is carved subtly into a runestone. Keep
Gather workers, three props, tracked chest, verification action and runestone
naturally to the right of centre. Docks, longhouse ribs, pines and dark water
form one continuous Valheim world across the panorama with coherent low-poly
geometry, weather and painterly light.
