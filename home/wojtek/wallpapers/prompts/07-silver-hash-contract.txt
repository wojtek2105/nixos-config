SCENE 07 — SILVER HASH CONTRACT

Original visual language: a high-quality The Witcher 3 game cinematic, not the
television adaptation and not generic fantasy. Show canonical game Ciri with
her recognizable face, ash-white hair, green eyes, facial scar, default game
wardrobe language and silver sword.

At a black marsh contract post, Ciri verifies a captured non-graphic monster
artifact before accepting payment. The silver sword edge carries a subtle
SHA-256 pattern that matches the wax seal on the contract. A brittle old seal
marked "MD5" is visibly cracked and rejected. Only after the strong match does
the artifact enter a physical quarantine cage. Keep text short and embedded in
the contract or seal.

Exactly one crossover: the contract folio contains a small hand-drawn sketch
of Frieren. Gather Ciri's complete head, sword, contract, both seals, cage and
sketch naturally to the right of centre. Dark reeds, water and alder roots form
one continuous Witcher 3 environment across the panorama with restrained
firelight and coherent depth.
