SCENE 09 — ZERO TRUST KEEP

Original visual language: The Witcher 3 game cinematic using canonical Ciri,
Yennefer and Geralt in their recognizable default game wardrobe and equipment.

Use three major props. One Authentik identity gate accepts Ciri's hardware MFA
key and issues Yennefer one short-lived violet seal with a visibly narrow scope.
That seal opens only one least-privilege corridor guarded by Geralt, then fades
and expires at its end. One stolen old seal fails at the identity gate. The
visible flow is only access request -> Authentik/MFA -> scoped short token ->
allowed corridor or expiry. Do not add Keycloak, Authelia, OAuth diagrams or a
catalog of identity products.

Exactly one crossover: the hardware key is engraved with Frieren's staff
silhouette. Gather the complete trio, identity gate, scoped corridor, failed
old seal and key naturally to the right of centre. The keep's halls, stairs and
barred recesses form one continuous architectural space across the panorama,
sharing perspective, material scale and practical light.
