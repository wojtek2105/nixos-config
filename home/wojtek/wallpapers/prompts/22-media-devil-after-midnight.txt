SCENE 22 — MEDIA DEVIL AFTER MIDNIGHT

Original visual language: unmistakable Chainsaw Man anime/manga cinematic with
canonical Makima, Denji and Power in default Public Safety wardrobe, exact
faces, hair, eyes and attitudes. Preserve kinetic dark comedy, urban grime and
dramatic manga composition without generic anime styling or graphic gore.

housing. Gather all three characters, reel, subtitle desk, projector, gauge and
