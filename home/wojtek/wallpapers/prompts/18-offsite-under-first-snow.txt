SCENE 18 — OFFSITE UNDER FIRST SNOW

Original visual language: authentic Valheim low-poly painterly game art with
rough timber, stone, cold weather, survival tools and original fully clothed
Viking characters. Richer light and texture may polish it, but preserve the
recognizable Valheim geometry and atmosphere.

Use exactly three major props. First, a Proxmox Backup Server loom accepts one
VM snapshot chest and visibly combines deduplication with a compact retention
rack. Second, a longship carries one encrypted offsite chest. Third, an isolated
test hut contains the restored VM miniature, one checksum seal and one service
lamp. An anxious archivist becomes relieved only when both seal and lamp turn
healthy. The visible flow is only snapshot -> PBS/retention -> offsite copy ->
isolated restore and service test. Short runes may read "PBS", "offsite",
"restore" and "OK" on physical objects. No extra backup copies, modern rack,
dashboard or untested decorative chest.

Exactly one crossover: Ciri's swallow medallion is carved on the offsite chest.
Gather characters, three props, tracked chest and carving naturally to the
right of centre. Snowy shore, longhouse and black pines form one continuous
Valheim landscape across the panorama with coherent low-poly geometry and
painterly winter light.
