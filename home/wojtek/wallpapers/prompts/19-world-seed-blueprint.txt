SCENE 19 — WORLD-SEED BLUEPRINT

Original visual language: unmistakable Valheim low-poly painterly rendering,
procedural terrain, rough building pieces, runestones, fog and original fully
clothed Vikings. Do not create a realistic Viking movie or generic fantasy art.

Use exactly three major props. First, one Git blueprint table combines a runic
state lock with the OpenTofu plan and approval seal. Second, one hammer applies
that plan into a Proxmox VM-shaped hut; a cloud-init scroll at its threshold
gives the first identity. Third, one Semaphore job board dispatches Ansible
workers to configure the interior, light the service hearth and later correct
the single drift board identified by a worried raven. The visible flow is only
Git/OpenTofu plan -> approved Proxmox VM -> Semaphore/Ansible -> healthy service
and drift fix.
Use labels only on those three props; no terminal, cloud logo or extra outpost.

Exactly one crossover: a small Pal Sphere is used as a paperweight on the
blueprint. Gather builders, three props, drift board, service hearth and sphere
naturally to the right of centre. The same terrain, outpost and forest form one
continuous Valheim landscape across the full panorama.
