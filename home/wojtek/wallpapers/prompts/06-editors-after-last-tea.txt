SCENE 06 — EDITORS AFTER LAST TEA

Original visual language: warm but very dark Frieren anime interior. Frieren,
Fern and Stark retain canonical faces, hair, default clothing and equipment.

In an old workshop, one failing remote ward needs the smallest safe config fix.
Frieren uses a universal iron key engraved "vi" because it is the only editor
guaranteed on the minimal host; a simple "nano" scroll nearby is the readable
rescue alternative. Fern then reproduces the change at her fast daily Neovim
workbench. One integrated mechanism performs LSP diagnosis, formatting and a
test before a physical diff page can be committed. The older Vim lineage is a
small engraving in the same workbench, not a separate competing product.

Two larger powered-down specialist lecterns marked "VS Code" and "JetBrains"
remain available for work that benefits from a GUI or deep language analysis,
but they do not interrupt the rescue-to-verified-commit flow. Show the choice
as honest tool fit: vi/nano for minimal rescue, Neovim as the quick keyboard-
first center, heavier IDEs for situational depth. No fanboy fight or logo wall.

Exactly one crossover: their tea infuser has Pochita's recognizable shape.
Gather the trio, remote ward, vi key, nano scroll, Neovim bench, diff and
infuser naturally to the right of centre. One timber workshop, its shelves,
archway and two subordinate dark lecterns continues across the entire panorama
with unbroken floor lines, perspective and warm practical light.
