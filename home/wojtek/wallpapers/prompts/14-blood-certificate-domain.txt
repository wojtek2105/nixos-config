SCENE 14 — BLOOD CERTIFICATE DOMAIN

Original visual language: unmistakable V Rising gameplay — stylized isometric
three-quarter camera, crimson-black Gothic palette, Castle Heart, blood essence,
patterned floor construction, servant coffins and domain management. Use an
original fully clothed player vampire; never create a generic vampire portrait,
photograph or unrelated castle.

Before dawn, use three major props. An outer ACME challenge gate validates one
domain token. A raven returns one complete chained certificate seal; one weak
intermediate link, not the leaf alone, is visibly red until replaced. The seal
then enters a Traefik ward marked "443", which terminates HTTPS for the castle.
One integrated status clock checks revocation state and triggers renewal before
expiry. The vampire moves from urgency at the broken chain to calm only when
the same Traefik gate turns healthy. The visible flow is ACME challenge -> full
chain -> Traefik HTTPS -> status and renewal. Use only "ACME", "chain",
"TRAEFIK" and "443" on physical objects; no other proxy or certificate UI.

Exactly one crossover: a small Pochita toy rests on the servant-management
desk. Gather vampire, Castle Heart, ACME path, chain, gate and toy naturally to
the right of centre. V Rising rooms, trellises and unlit vaults form one
continuous isometric domain across the entire panorama.
