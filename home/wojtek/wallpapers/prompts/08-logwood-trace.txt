SCENE 08 — LOGWOOD TRACE

Original visual language: The Witcher 3 game cinematic with canonical game
Yennefer and Geralt, never television likenesses. Preserve Yennefer's raven
curls, violet eyes, black-and-white default wardrobe and obsidian star;
preserve Geralt's white hair, scars, cat eyes, armor, medallion and two swords.

In a dark forest, one failing request begins as a tiny OpenTelemetry trace-id
rune. It binds three types of spoor: Prometheus metric beads, Loki log cuts in
bark and a Tempo trace thread. One compact Grafana-like alchemical instrument
correlates them into the same narrow gate; a small Sentry case folio records the
resulting exception and context. Yennefer identifies the real bottleneck,
Geralt removes it, then the same instrument turns healthy green. A loud red
alert leading to an empty hollow is the false lead, so volume alone never wins.
This is one request-to-diagnosis-to-fix-to-verification story, not five logos.

Exactly one crossover: a small recognizable Pal plush is tied behind Roach's
saddle. Gather Yennefer, Geralt, the three signals, false lead, real bottleneck
and plush naturally to the right of centre. Twisted trunks and roots continue
through one uninterrupted Witcher 3 forest panorama with consistent materials,
fog, perspective and camera language.
